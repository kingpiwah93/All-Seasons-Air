About All Seasons Air

The people and passion behind Gauteng's most trusted HVAC company.

Our Story

Built on Quality, Driven by Service

Founded in 2010, All Seasons Air began with a simple mission: to bring world-class climate control solutions to homes and businesses across Gauteng. What started as a small team of passionate engineers in Midrand has grown into one of the province's most respected HVAC companies.

Our founder saw a gap in the market for a company that combined technical excellence with genuine customer care. All Seasons Air was born to fill that gap, and today we serve thousands of residential and commercial clients across Johannesburg, Pretoria, and the greater Gauteng region.

Our team of SAQCC-registered technicians undergoes continuous training to stay ahead of industry developments and deliver the latest energy-efficient solutions.
